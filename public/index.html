Time to finality — lower is better (log scale)

Chains — sampled every 12 seconds

How to read this: block time is measured between this monitor's own consecutive samples (some chains cluster block timestamps, which makes historical windows lie). Finality is the gap between the latest head and the finalized head (latest − finalized), converted to seconds with that chain's own block time. Portaldot's finality is cross-checked two ways — the EVM finalized tag and the native Substrate finalized head. Gas is the RPC-reported price in wei. Reliability is the share of successful polls per endpoint since this monitor started; dropped requests are retried with backoff before a poll counts as failed. ChainLens only reads public RPCs — no wallet, no keys, no transactions.
